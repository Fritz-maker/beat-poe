## common.txt

The words of `words.txt` that are common in everyday English: those with a
Zipf frequency of 2.0 or more according to `wordfreq` by Robyn Speer
(https://github.com/rspeer/wordfreq).

Because this list was made with wordfreq's data, it is shared under the same
terms as that data: the Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0
International license (https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/).

wordfreq's data draws on these sources, which it asks users to credit:

- Google Books Ngrams (http://books.google.com/ngrams) and Google Books Syntactic Ngrams
- The Leeds Internet Corpus, University of Leeds Centre for Translation Studies
- Wikipedia, the free encyclopedia (http://www.wikipedia.org)
- ParaCrawl, a multilingual web crawl (https://paracrawl.eu)
- OPUS OpenSubtitles 2018, with data from the OpenSubtitles project (http://www.opensubtitles.org/)
- The SUBTLEX word lists (SUBTLEX-US, SUBTLEX-UK, SUBTLEX-CH, SUBTLEX-DE, SUBTLEX-NL),
  created by Marc Brysbaert et al. SUBTLEX is freely available data:
  http://crr.ugent.be/programs-data/subtitle-frequencies
- Word statistics collected from Twitter's streaming API

Citation: Robyn Speer. (2022). rspeer/wordfreq: v3.0 (v3.0.2). Zenodo. https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.7199437
