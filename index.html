How to play

  1. Tap tiles to spell a word of 3+ letters, then tap Play.
  2. Long words and rare letters hit much harder.
  3. Every few turns a curse wave turns tiles into hard letters.
  4. Stuck? Scramble deals a whole new grid and clears cursed tiles, but the foe gets a free hit. Tap it twice to confirm.
  5. Play the best word the grid allows to earn a bonus life for the final fight.
